# Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)

This document analyzes potential system, operational, and human failure modes in the spare-parts demand planning and override workflow.

| Failure Mode | Root Cause | System Impact | Detection Mechanism | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Incorrect Baseline Forecast** | Statistical moving average lagging during sudden failure spikes or intermittent zero demand patterns. | Under-budgeted inventory, stockout risk, or excess holding cost. | Baseline MAE tracking and high error alerts in evaluation engine. | Allow domain expert planner overrides and incorporate ML models (Random Forest). |
| **Bad Planner Override** | Speculative or emotional overriding by planner without field evidence (e.g. over-adjusting R04 or R06). | Degraded demand accuracy, stockouts, or artificial demand inflation. | Override outcome evaluation (`Override Error > Baseline Error`) and learning module ranking. | Restrict authorization limits based on planner role and flag underperforming reason codes for review. |
| **Missing Override Reason** | Planner rushes form submission without entering required reason code. | Lack of auditability, inability to learn from historical overrides. | Hard constraint validator (`validate_hard_constraints`). | System REJECTS submission and blocks form entry until reason code R01–R10 is selected. |
| **Supplier Capacity Exceeded** | Override quantity pushes final demand beyond maximum supplier production volume. | Unfillable orders, supplier breach, delayed equipment repair. | Hard constraint validator comparing Final Demand against Supplier Capacity. | System REJECTS override submission and alerts planner with exact capacity shortfall. |
| **Driver Overload / Unsafe Hours** | Emergency dispatch assigned to driver who has exceeded 100% safe working hours. | High risk of road accidents, fatigue violations, driver burnout. | Driver safety workload check (`Assigned Hours / Maximum Safe Hours`). | System REJECTS dispatch and flags safety status as `UNSAFE / REJECT`. Reassigns order to safe driver. |
| **Unexpected Demand Spike** | Catastrophic multi-equipment failure or regional environmental disruption. | Rapid stockout, emergency freight costs, service level drop. | Demand spike detector (`Actual Demand > 3x Historical Demand`). | Trigger multi-objective optimization (Strategy B: Reliability-focused) and buffer safety stock. |
| **Bad / Corrupted Actual Demand** | Manual entry error or duplicated receipt logs in inventory ERP. | Distorted outcome measurement and faulty historical learning. | Preprocessing outlier cleaning and sanity validation bounds. | Impute missing values, flag statistical outliers, and maintain raw backup tables. |
| **Data Quality Issue (Missing Values)** | Incomplete field comments, missing delivery distances, unassigned vehicle types. | Partial delivery calculation failure or inaccurate emission estimates. | Automated data cleaning and imputation module (`src/preprocessing.py`). | Fill missing comments with default text and impute numerical fields with regional medians. |
